\section{Project Overview}
The goal of the project is to design and implement a system for managing a car rental company. The application should support customer registration and profile editing, browsing available cars, making rental reservations, reviewing rental history, generating reports, online payments and using discount coupons. On the company side, the system should support fleet management, customer management, rental settlement, invoice generation and informing regular customers about promotions and discounts.

\section{Database Diagram}
The current database design is centered around the \texttt{RENTAL} table, which connects customers, cars, pickup and return locations. Financial settlement is represented separately by the \texttt{INVOICE} table, while vehicle attributes, rental states and payment data are normalized through dedicated lookup entities.

\midrule
\endhead
\texttt{CUSTOMER} & Stores personal, contact, authentication and driving license data required for rentals. \\
\texttt{ADDRESS} & Stores reusable address data used by customers and rental locations. \\
\texttt{LOCATION} & Represents rental locations with contact data and an address, used as car storage, pickup and return places. \\
\texttt{CAR} & Stores vehicle identity, descriptive attributes, mileage, daily rate, current location and references to vehicle lookup entities. \\
\texttt{FUEL\_TYPE} & Lookup entity describing the fuel type assigned to a car. \\
\texttt{TRANSMISSION} & Lookup entity describing the transmission type assigned to a car. \\
\texttt{CAR\_TYPE} & Lookup entity classifying cars by body or business category. \\
\texttt{CAR\_STATUS} & Lookup entity describing the operational status of a car, for example available or unavailable. \\
\texttt{RENTAL} & Central transactional entity describing reservation and rental execution, including customer, car, pickup and return locations, status and rental dates. \\
\texttt{RENTAL\_STATUS} & Lookup entity describing the state of a rental. \\
\texttt{INVOICE} & Stores settlement data for a rental, including invoice number, amounts before and after discount, issue date, payment method, payment status and payment timestamp. \\
\texttt{PAYMENT\_STATUS} & Lookup entity describing whether an invoice payment is pending, completed, failed or otherwise classified. \\
\texttt{PAYMENT\_METHOD} & Lookup entity describing the payment method used for an invoice. \\
\texttt{DISCOUNT} & Stores discount codes and percentage-based promotions with validity dates and active flag; applied to invoices when used. \\
