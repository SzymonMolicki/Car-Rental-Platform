\section{Project Overview}
The goal of the project is to design and implement a system for managing a car rental company. The application should support customer registration and profile editing, browsing available cars, making rental reservations, reviewing rental history, generating reports, online payments and using discount coupons. On the company side, the system should support fleet management, customer management, rental settlement, invoice generation and informing regular customers about promotions and discounts.

\section{Core Entities}
\begin{table}[h!]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}p{2.7cm}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Entity} & \textbf{Role in the system} \\
\midrule
\texttt{CUSTOMER} & Stores personal, contact, and driving license data required for rentals. \\
\texttt{LOCATION} & Represents rental branches used as car storage points and pickup or return places. \\
\texttt{CAR} & Stores vehicle identity, characteristics, status, rate, mileage and current location. \\
\texttt{DISCOUNT} & Stores discount codes and percentage-based promotions with validity dates. \\
\texttt{RENTAL} & Central transactional entity describing reservation and rental execution, cost calculation, invoice data and payment status. \\
